# CANONICAL RESEARCH SCHEMA

Required provenance fields: source, source_url, competition, country, season, match_id, home_team, away_team, kickoff_time, result, market, odds, timestamp, data_type, provenance.

PIT statuses: PIT_EXACT, PIT_APPROXIMATE, PIT_DATE_ONLY, NON_PIT, UNKNOWN.

Evidence classes: HISTORICAL_REAL, HISTORICAL_REAL_NON_PIT, LIVE_REAL, DEMO, MOCK, SYNTHETIC.

No DEMO/MOCK/SYNTHETIC record may enter empirical evidence tables. Missing values remain NULL.
