# ROBO DA BET V20

V20 is an incremental evolution of V19. It preserves the V19 pricing/PIT/research surfaces and adds:

- selective global opportunity ranking and NO BET reasons;
- configurable minimum odds (1.50 default) and preferred odds (1.66);
- fractional Kelly stake sizing with uncertainty/correlation reduction;
- portfolio/event/day/simultaneous exposure limits;
- live state repricing with fail-closed sample gate;
- immutable paper ledger with settlement and XLSX export;
- today/month/all performance, league/market aggregation;
- optional Telegram provider abstraction;
- explicit PAPER/SHADOW boundary and no bookmaker execution path.

Scientific status remains dependent on real PIT historical odds. DEMO fixtures do not establish edge, ROI, CLV or OOS profitability.
